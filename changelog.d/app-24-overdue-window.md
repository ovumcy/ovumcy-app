### Changed

- **Late-cycle predictions match the web app.** The "cycle data may be outdated" state and the point where predictions are withheld now start from the shorter of the projected and average cycle lengths. In the week after a missed period the dashboard shows the actual cycle day as "~N" instead of restarting at day 1, and the calendar shows only the missed period — no later predicted cycles or fertile windows. A BBT-confirmed ovulation day stays on the calendar after predictions are withheld.
