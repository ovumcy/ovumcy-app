### Fixed

- **Today stays today overnight.** The dashboard and calendar pick up the new day when you return to them, when the app comes back to the foreground, or at midnight while they stay open, so an entry made after midnight is no longer filed under yesterday. An unsaved edit is saved on its own day first.
- **Contraction history shows the right day** in time zones west of Greenwich.
- **Early-bleeding notice follows prediction pauses.** It no longer appears during an active pregnancy, after a positive test, or in unpredictable-cycle mode.
- **Reminders fail safe.** If reminder data cannot be read, scheduled reminders are cleared until the next successful update instead of staying armed.
- **Partner view updates right away** when a pregnancy is started, ended or deleted.
