### Fixed

- **Pregnancy and postpartum data can be reached from Settings.** While any pregnancy, postpartum or check-in data is saved, Settings shows a "Pregnancy and postpartum" row that opens the manage screen, so postpartum tracking can still be started after "Not now" within eight weeks of the birth, and saved data can be managed or deleted from cycle mode.
- **Clearer pregnancy and postpartum wording.** Recording a birth no longer promises a return to cycle tracking before the postpartum offer; the manage screen is titled for postpartum while it is active; the end-postpartum dialog drops its trailing "you can do this later".
- **Postpartum check-in dates read as dates.** The last check-in and the check-in history show a localized date instead of a raw ISO date, and the intro and history screens no longer repeat their heading.
- **Web accessibility.** Buttons carry their label as an accessible name, and toggles, chips, choice options and calendar days expose their checked or selected state on the web build.
