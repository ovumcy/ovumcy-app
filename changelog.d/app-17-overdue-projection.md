### Fixed

- **A late period keeps its own date.** Once the expected day has passed, the dashboard, the partner view and the upcoming-period reminder no longer jump to a date a whole cycle later: the next period stays the date it was due, shown as approximate. Once the cycle runs more than a week past its length, no next-period, ovulation or phase estimate is shown until a new period is logged. Prediction vectors match ovumcy-web again.
