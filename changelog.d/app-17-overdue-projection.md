### Fixed

- **A late period keeps its own date.** Once the expected day has passed, the dashboard, the partner view and the upcoming-period reminder no longer jump to a date a whole cycle later: the next period stays the date it was due, shown as approximate. Once the cycle runs more than a week past its length, no next-period, ovulation or phase estimate is shown and the calendar paints no predicted period or fertile days until a new period is logged; the cycle day then counts on from the last logged period instead of restarting. Prediction vectors match ovumcy-web again.
