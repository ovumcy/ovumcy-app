### Added

- **Cycle ribbon on Today, as on ovumcy-web.** Under the ring, one cell per day of the running
  cycle shows its phase, the fertile window, the predicted period and start window, and the days
  already logged, with a key. Below three completed cycles the days past the period read
  "Fertile details held back". Settings → Interface → Cycle map turns the ring and
  the ribbon on or off (both on by default; turning both off keeps the ribbon).
