### Fixed

- **The cycle card no longer asks for a cycle start while predictions are paused.** It says the
  predictions are paused, and the ring shows a dash instead of a word that broke across lines on
  narrow screens.
- **After a pregnancy ends, the paused predictions are explained without the positive test.** After
  a loss, a birth or another ending, the dashboard and the save message say that predictions stay
  paused until your next period. The loss and "something else" screens no longer show the pregnancy
  timing footer.
- **Screen readers no longer announce a raw label on confirmation dialogs.** The dimmed background
  behind a dialog is no longer read out. Back or the dialog's own buttons close it.
