### Fixed

- **Recovery codes and phrases are no longer lost to the Back button.** On Android, Back used to close the one-time recovery code dialog after registration or a guest upgrade as if you had saved it, discarding the only copy. Back now leaves the dialog open, and only the confirm button closes it. Confirming a new recovery phrase now hides the words everywhere on screen, and a changed Ovumcy Cloud phrase you have confirmed is never shown again.
- **Confirmation dialogs show Cancel in your language.** Disconnect, restore, delete-account and recovery-phrase dialogs used to show an English "Cancel" in every language.
