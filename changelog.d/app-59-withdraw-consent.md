### Added

- **Withdraw data-sharing consent in one tap.** While a consent for sync or partner sharing is stored, Backup & Sync shows a "Withdraw consent" control. One tap clears every stored consent, and the screen says what stops (new backups and partner updates) and what stays (account, server backup, the partner's last copy). The next send asks for consent again. The consent dialog now mentions this control in all six languages.
