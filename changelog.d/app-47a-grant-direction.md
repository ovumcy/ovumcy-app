### Fixed

- **A renewed partner share opens again.** When you re-share with the same partner, their app no longer rejects every update until your new share catches up with the old one; it starts fresh under the new invite. If your partner opens the old share, they are asked to open your new invite link.
- **Re-sharing from this device updates the partner again.** A share renewed from this device now switches to the new invite's key instead of asking you to revoke and invite once more.

### Security

- **Shares you received are never sent back.** This device remembers which side of each share it holds, so a share you received can never be used to upload your own data, whatever the server lists.
- **Revoked shares you received are forgotten.** When the owner revokes a share you received, this device deletes its key for it.
